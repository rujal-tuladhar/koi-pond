Koi Pond: Feed the Fish

A calm little koi pond for kids. Tap the water, drop some food and watch the fish swim over and gobble it up.

Common questions

How do I play? Tap anywhere on the water to drop food. Tap "Pick your fish" to choose which koi live in your pond and the water color.

There's no sound. Koi Pond follows your silent switch. Turn the switch off, raise the volume, and check that Sounds is on in "Pick your fish".

Does it need the internet? No. Koi Pond works fully offline.

Does it collect any data? No. See our privacy policy.
